1.3.0-patches Mechanics Phase 1 · Differ Shipped in live `1.3.0-patches`

Three food spots

Pellets still drop on the same clock (Food spawn interval). They just don’t land evenly. Three circles stay put for a run. Most drops land inside them. A few still land anywhere so the rest of the island isn’t a dead zone.

What you should see

Open Grazers vs Hunters, Reset, watch. Camps form on the three piles. Wanderers who miss the circles starve sooner. Seekers walk between piles. The thin land still gets a trickle — not a hard wall.

Where the circles sit

Island is the live canvas (about 800×600, it grows if you hide columns). Spots are fractions of the map, so they stay in the same neighborhoods when the canvas resizes. Triangle layout: two up, one down. They don’t overlap.

Gaps between centers are bigger than two radii, so the circles don’t eat each other. People still wrap at the edges like today.

How a drop works

Same as now: every Interval ticks, drop 3 pellets (Reset still seeds a pile). Then, for each pellet:
  1. 80% pick one of the three spots at random, then a random point inside that circle.
  2. 20% anywhere on the island (today’s rule).
Total food per minute does not change. Only where it lands.
Spots are picked once at Reset and stay put until the next Reset. First version: same three fractions every run (what this drawing is). Tiny jitter can wait until the camps feel too samey.
