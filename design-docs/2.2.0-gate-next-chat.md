## What 2.2.0-gate is

Today a person births once age hits `LIFE_RISE` (3600 ticks, about 60 seconds at 1×), birth frequency is above about 0.04, and their own gap has passed. Hunger does not jump when a kid appears. The only cost is the starter bag (about 1–4 food, taken off the parent). That is why a high birth-frequency line can flood the island.

Change only the gate:

- Still has to be past prime, birth frequency above the ghost floor, and the personal gap elapsed.
- Also has to have bag at least a small floor and hunger under a ceiling.
- Starting numbers to try, not a formula: **bag at least 2, hunger under 70**. I will pick the real numbers by watching.
- Missing the bag or hunger check does **not** reset the birth clock. They try again on later ticks.
- Do not add a hunger bill, an extra pellet, a wider nudge, or a snack cutoff in this version. Those are later slices.

What I should see: a Mayfly-style high birth with a bad bag stops flooding. A slow line with a fat bag still has kids. If nobody births, the floor is too high. If the flood continues, the floor is too low. Say that in plain words and let me watch. Do not quietly retune hunger rate, speed, spawn, or bump.
