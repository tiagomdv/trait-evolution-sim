First visible Evolve — kids beside a living parent

Copy-four-traits + Reset is Differ with a new Mix. You never see a parent. This slice keeps adults on the island and spawns kids next to them. Mix now has five knobs: hunger rate, efficiency, hunt, speed, and birth frequency. Cling / care clocks are still not Mix. Help / Vision leftover cleanup is a later pass.

What you should see

Open Mix, Apply, Reset. For about one minute at 1× it looks like Differ. Then small dots appear next to living people. They stick, then peel toward pellets. Click a small one: name ends in kid, bag is tiny, Cling shows in the inspector and falls toward zero.

Clocks vs Mix traits

Kids spawn with full Mix traits (copied + nudge). No weak-body curve. Clocks only change who hunt points at: parent, newborn, then food.

How a kid is made

  1. Each tick, after deaths: living bodies older than LIFE_RISE whose Mix birth-frequency gap has elapsed can have a kid. No live head-count cap.
  2. Same parent can have more than one kid. Too-young bodies do not birth. Gap is Mix birth frequency.
  3. Kid appears 8–18 world units beside the parent (wraps if they sit on the edge).
  4. Paint and Mix group stay the parent’s. Name is parent name + " kid".
  5. Mix traits copied from the parent, then a small nudge, clamped to Mix ranges. Full strength from tick one.
  6. Bag starts ~1–4. Hunger starts ~22–40 so they actually hunt. Adults keep the bags they already had.
Nudge spans: hunger rate ±0.004, efficiency ±0.08, hunt ±0.06, speed ±0.1. Mix Reset adults still spawn the old way: random place, bag ~18–30, hunger 0.

Parent-pull (cling) and care (parent → kid)

Hunt already steers. Extra is state, not a Mix slider.

Cling (kid): lock on that parent. Starts at 1. Each tick: 1 − age / LIFE_RISE, minus hunger/100. Hunger ≥ 55 hunts pellets even if cling is left (empty-bag kids were dying in the clump). Parent dead → food. No ghost.

Care (parent): for a while after a birth, the same lock aims at the newest living kid. Clock starts at lastBirthTick (a new baby resets it). Same length as cling. Hunger ≥ 55 → pellets first. Newest kid dead → next living offspring while the clock is up, else food. No ghost.

Hunt order: care (newborn) first, then own parent, then food. So a parent who is also someone’s kid will tend the baby before chasing grandma. Hunt 0 still barely locks — wanderer families do not glue. That is hunt, not a bug.

What you should see: after a birth they meet in a clump (no bump tax on that pair). Then both peel toward pellets. Next baby, the parent’s hunt snaps to the new one; an older kid may still be clinging if their own clock is up.

No life curve

Parked. Kids are the parent’s Mix numbers plus a nudge. Empty bag is the only “baby” tell (small dot).
