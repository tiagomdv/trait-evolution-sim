Help · how the lab works

Agents get hungrier over time. If hunger hits the limit, they die. They pick up food from the ground and can later eat what they carry. Your job is to change the world and watch who lasts.

How agents move
Seek — The agent looks for nearby food and walks toward it. Hungrier agents try harder (see Seek strength). There is still a little random wobble so motion doesn’t look robotic.
Random — The agent just wanders. No aiming at food. Use this as a baseline: with the same hunger and food settings, Random should usually do worse than Seek.
Quick A/B: leave presets alone, run Seek for a bit, switch to Random, watch deaths climb. That’s the point of the toggle.
Main controls
Initial agents — How many start when you hit Reset. Changing this mid-run does nothing until the next Reset.
Hunger rate — How fast hunger climbs. Higher = harsher world; agents need food more often.
Food spawn interval — How long between new food drops. Higher number = food is rarer = harder.
Sense radius — How far a seeking agent can notice food. If food is farther than this, they can’t home in on it (they just wander until something comes into range).
Seek strength — Only matters in Seek mode. It controls how strongly hunger turns into “I need to go get that food.” Full and not hungry → mostly stroll. Very hungry → race toward the nearest pellet they can see. Turn this up and even medium hunger makes them commit hard. Turn it down (or to zero) and they barely seek even when starving.
Think of hunger as the need, and seek strength as how loud that need is when steering. The inspector’s “seek blend” is “how hard is this one agent seeking right now?”
Difficulty presets
Lenient — Soft world. Good for demos and checking that Seek looks right.
Balanced — Default lab. Real pressure, not instant extinction.
Punishing — Harsh stress test. Only careful Seek + luck keeps a remnant; Random dies fast.
Presets set hunger, food spawn, and the advanced eating knobs together. They do not switch Seek/Random for you. If you drag a linked slider, the active preset clears to custom.
Advanced eating (popup)
Ground eat relief — How much hunger drops when they eat food off the ground.
Self-consume threshold — Hunger must get this high before they start eating food they already carry.
Self-consume amount — How much carried food they nibble each step when self-eating.
Self-consume relief — How much hunger that nibble removes.
Ground food is the big rescue. Carried food is a backup snack once they’re already quite hungry.
Live numbers (left dock)
Alive / Deaths — Who’s still up · how many starved this run.
Food — Pellets currently on the map (not what’s in pockets).
Avg hunger — Average hunger of living agents. Rising = population is getting desperate.
Min / Max food — Poorest and richest living agent by carried food (inventory). Not min/max hunger.
Avg food / agent — Average carried food. Low average + high hunger = bad time coming.
Special agent (different rules)
Special is one amber-marked agent that can use its own parameters (policy, sense, seek, hunger, eat) while everyone else uses the world controls. Open Special… to toggle “custom” per knob or Match all → world.
Pin is different — pin only chooses who you watch on trends. Special chooses who plays by different rules. You usually pin the special when testing overrides.
Killer demo: set world policy to Random, open Special…, custom policy = Seek. The amber agent should still path to food while the crowd wanders.
Focus / pin · Reroll body — inside the Special popup: re-select and pin the special, or move the special profile to another living agent.
Selected agent
Click someone on the canvas. You’ll see their id, hunger, carried food, effective policy (world or special), how hard they’re seeking, and distance to food if they can sense any. Pin to trends keeps their hunger/food charts on the right after you click away.
History (right column)
The history strip shows the latest 3 finished runs (kept compact so trends have room). Open All history… for the full session table in a popup.
Pre — Which difficulty preset that finished run used (or custom).
Dur — How long the run lasted.
End — How many were still alive at the end.
Hung / Food — Average hunger and average carried food over that run. Useful when comparing Seek vs Random or preset ladders.
