Vision · trait-evolution-sim

Dots walk an island, get hungry, and eat pellets. Some last; some don’t. Live is 1.4.1-camera — people are not all the same (Phase 1 · Differ).

Help · Differ World

Dots walk a 2D island. Hunger goes up every tick. Past 100 they die. Grab a pellet → one food in the bag. They nibble the bag when hungry enough. People are not copies of each other. You set the mix in Mix. Version 1.4.1-camera.

How a run works
Open Mix. Groups — kinds, everyone in a kind is the same. Families — the same mixes, but each named person sits a little off the group average. Rungs — hunt/hunger/efficiency/speed ladders, a scramble, or 12 oddballs. Groups and Roster tabs are separate drafts. A short story sits under the buttons.
Press Apply to remember the open tab. The island does not change yet.
Press Reset to spawn that mix. That is when colors and counts on the field update.
Food spawn on the left is weather (live). Gap between pellet drops, 1–20. Higher = scarcer. 8 is already a hungry island at 60 people. 1 is almost a pellet every tick. (Zero is not a real gap — it would break the clock.)
Three spots (checkbox, live) — most pellets land in three piles (dashed rings). A few still fall anywhere. Uncheck for even rain like before.
The island is a zoom-out: the box is the same size, people and pellets look smaller, walks take longer. World size locks on first layout. Hide Mix / Hide graphs only change the camera — the view fills left to right. Hide may crop top and bottom; putting a bar back shows the whole map again. Same land, no extra strip, no wrap.
Trends: alive, avg bag, total bag (sum of bags in that color), avg hunger. One line per group.
Pause / Speed — clock. 10× is faster wall time; a sim-second is still 60 ticks.
Watch leftover colors. Score who is still walking, not how fat the bags are. Right side: a graph per group, and a history row when you Reset or when everyone dies.
The four traits
Hunger rate — How fast they burn. High = starve sooner if they miss food.
Efficiency — How much a bite from the bag lowers hunger. High = better meals.
Hunt — One number for “do I lock onto food?” Zero is basically off (wander). Around the middle is normal seeking. One is fully locked on. Extra hunt past that barely saves more lives.
Speed — How fast they can walk. Slow is a tax. Faster is not always more survivors — it is there so a mix can look slow vs twitchy.
What you see
Click a person — name, four traits, bag.
Fill color is paint for the whole group (not the group’s name). Pink ring = starving. Cyan pip = food in the bag.
Total people is the sum of group counts (or roster length). Cap 100.
Import / Export still work if you want a JSON file. Stories live in Mix; the files in the folder are extras.
Rules
Catch = bag +1, pellet gone. Reach is 20 pixels past the drawn circle. Dots grow with the bag (still climbing after 1000–2000 pellets). Fat leftovers are slower — same speed trait, but the extra mass drags them. If several people reach the same pellet, a random one in range eats it.
Bite size from the bag still uses a frozen “how big a mouthful” number times efficiency. Efficiency is the Differ trait; mouthful size is not.
Trends and History
Trends — one colored line per kind: alive, average bag, average hunger. Left edge is the start of the run (time 0). Bottom is 0. Alive goes to zero when a kind is gone; bag and hunger keep the last living average.
History — one row per finished run (Reset or wipe). Leftover count plus a chip per group. Best this session is the run that left the most people, not the one that lasted longest.
