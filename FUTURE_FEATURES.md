## Differ — when we come back

The World already has a crowd: Crowd…, Apply, Reset, four traits, caste numbers on the left. What’s missing is the rest of the *lab* around that crowd.

**Trends and History that know groups.**  
Those two right-hand panels are still Survive’s (one line for “the population”). We veiled them so nobody thinks they explain a mixed field. When we turn them back on, a trend should be able to show caste A vs caste B, and a history row should remember the crowd you Applied — not only “60 in, 4 out.”

**Lab that can mix types.**  
Survive Lab is great at “everyone is X.” Differ’s question is “half X, half Y — who lasts?” Leave Lab off until it can import a Crowd JSON and score *who* lived, not only how many.

**Special…**  
A single pet with overrides made sense when the world was uniform. Next to Crowd… it is a second way to make someone different. Stay off until we actually want “pin this one body” again.

**Worth a quiet afternoon, not a phase**

Confirm hunt’s decode with a few stamped Crowds (hunt 0, mid, high) instead of trusting only the old Seek-master sweep. Don’t let a random roster sit half the people under hunger ~0.049 unless the story is “almost nobody can die.” A World seed in the export would make a nice run replayable; Lab already has seeds.

Leave patience, following, hoarding, and trade-boldness alone. They need new rules or Economy.

## Why hunt is one number (and speed is still there)

We did not invent the trait list. We sat in Survive Lab, one slider at a time, and watched leftover population at 90 seconds. The README section *Why the leftover four* is the human version.

The one thing that still surprises people: Lab cannot see *inside* a remnant. Speed barely changed how many lived, but in a mixed field the quicker ones may still be the ones standing. That’s why speed stayed as a Crowd trait.

## After Differ

**Evolve** is kids, inheritance, mutation — watch a trait move across generations. A fun extra later: take whoever is left, mutate a little, Reset (“what wins next year”). Not before you can see a birth.

**Economy** starts with nearby trade, then storage, then making things. One piece at a time, and only once Evolve has something to look at.

## Survive leftovers (closed on purpose)

None of this is a reason to reopen the Survive file. If we want it, it comes back as Differ World/Lab or as a Playbook.

A **Playbook** would be recipes we actually ran (what we set, what we watched, why it was interesting) — not a second Help and not a second roadmap.

When Lab is Differ-aware, cheap polish: don’t average cancelled trials, keep the last few batches, maybe a graph. Survive Lab in the finish file is already enough for uniform worlds.

World and Lab still don’t share one step function, so they can drift. Fair-eat is in live World; Survive Lab still gives the pellet to whoever is first in the array. Matching canvas size to Lab’s 800×600 only matters if we claim Lab is a copy of the picture. Saving history to disk is later; export is enough.

We parked, and are not queuing: memory of the last meal, a food-density gradient, sliding column drawers, named Survive “story” presets. We will not bring back multi-size pellets or chew-until-empty. A catch is +1 in the bag and the pellet is gone.
